Yuen Yuen Ang is one of the most thoughtful commentators on all things China, and everything she writes—including her books—is a must-read if you wish to understand the phenomenon that is China. She just made her book, *[How China Escaped the Poverty Trap](https://cornellpress.cornell.edu/book/9781501705854/how-china-escaped-the-poverty-trap/#bookTabs=1)*, free and openly downloadable.

As I've mentioned, I have an enduring fascination with China, and much of what I know about it is informed by the mental models and heuristics from Yuen Yuen Ang's work. I highly recommend downloading the book and reading it.
