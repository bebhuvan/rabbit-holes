So true: 

> *"**To poison a nation, poison its stories. A demoralised nation tells demoralised stories to itself.** Beware of the storytellers who are not fully conscious of the importance of their gifts, and who are irresponsible in the application of their art: they could unwittingly help along the psychic destruction of their people."* [[1](https://www.facebook.com/Chalewotefest/posts/to-poison-a-nation-poison-its-stories-a-demoralised-nation-tells-demoralised-sto/1257037206567996/), [2](https://www.azquotes.com/author/11059-Ben_Okri)]

This quote originates from the book ++***A Way of Being Free*++ (1997)**, a collection of essays and aphorisms written by the Booker Prize-winning Nigerian author ++**[Sir Ben Okri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Okri)**++.
