What a legend. Came across the name for the first time in *[India after Gandhi](https://www.amazon.in/India-After-Gandhi-History-Democracy/dp/9382616977):* 

> With the enactment of the *[Government of India Act 1935](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India_Act_1935 "Government of India Act 1935")*, the couple joined the [Muslim League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-India_Muslim_League "All-India Muslim League") and entered electoral politics. In the 1937 elections, she was one of the few women who successfully contested from a non-reserved seat and was elected to the U.P legislative assembly. Begum Aijaz Rasul remained a member until 1952. She held the office of the Deputy President of the council from 1937 to 1940 and acted as the Leader of Opposition in the council from 1950 to 1952–54. She was the first woman in India and the first Muslim woman in the world to reach this position. Despite her family background, she was known for her strong support for [zamindari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamindar "Zamindar") abolition. She also strongly opposed the demand for separate electorates based on religion.
>
> In 1946, she was elected to the Constituent Assembly of India and was one of 28 Muslim League members who finally joined. She was the only Muslim woman in the Assembly. In 1950, the Muslim League in India dissolved and Begum Aijaz Rasul joined the Congress. She was elected to the [Rajya Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajya_Sabha "Rajya Sabha") in 1952-54 and was a member of the Uttar Pradesh Legislative Assembly from 1969 to 1989.
>
> Between 1969 and 1971, she was the Minister for Social Welfare and Minorities. In 2000, she was awarded a [Padma Bhushan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Bhushan "Padma Bhushan") for her contribution to social work.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Begum_Aizaz_Rasul#cite_note-Padma_Awards-2)
