>  Smith was arrested in 2024 on a three-count indictment that charged him with using thousands of bots to continuously stream songs on Spotify, Apple Music and other platforms starting in 2017. Prosecutors claimed he initially started by merely boosting the plays of his own music, then switched to mass-producing AI songs to fuel the scheme.
>
> In March, he entered a guilty plea on a single count of wire fraud conspiracy and agreed to forfeit more than $8 million in streaming fraud proceeds. That charge carries a five-year maximum prison sentence, and federal guidelines call for between 46 and 57 months, but probation officials recommended a lighter sentence of 24 months.
