This has been my experience with LLMs as well.  
I often see posts from people with fancy, complex setups for coding with AI, claiming that doing X or Y leads to some ridiculous output. It used to make me feel a bit jealous—like I was missing out or using these tools completely wrong.

But whenever I actually sit down and use these coding tools, even with the most basic approach, the thing I set out to build eventually gets done. More often than not, I stick to projects I think are important or fun, and things just work out.  
This post was a nice reminder of that whole dynamic

> I often think about that story when browsing X. Every day, someone invents a new way of working that promises to change everything. Some probably will. But ultimately what matters most is solving the right problems.
