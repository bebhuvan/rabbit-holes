Yawp is a simple voice-typing tool I vibe-coded for my own workflow. Over the last few months, I’ve found myself leaning heavily into voice input—to the point where typing on a traditional keyboard is starting to feel completely anachronistic.

I’ve been dogfooding it constantly, and unlike most weekend side projects that get built and forgotten, Yawp has become a daily driver for me.

Recently, I had Codex take a pass at optimizing the codebase. It pushed a series of under-the-hood changes—most of which I don't fully understand—but the result is noticeably faster and far more accurate transcription.

A few quick details:

- **Linux-only:** Built specifically for Linux desktop environments.
- **100% Local:** Runs models locally on your machine, so there are no subscriptions, API keys, or third-party cloud dependencies.

If you're on Linux and want a lightweight, local-first way to voice-type, give it a spin: 

[https://github.com/bebhuvan/yawp](https://github.com/bebhuvan/yawp)
