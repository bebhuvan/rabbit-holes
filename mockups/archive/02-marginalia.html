We are living in wild times

There's something almost surreal about the moment we're in. A few years ago, the idea of talking to a computer and having it build you a website, write you a poem, or help you think through a thorny philosophical question was science fiction. Now it's Tuesday.

I recently built this entire blog by talking to an AI. Not writing code — talking. Describing what I wanted, arguing with it, watching it conjure things from plain English. We really are living in wild times.
