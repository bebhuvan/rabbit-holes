¶ 366 Verse 21 Sep 2025

Wild Geese

You do not have to be good.
You do not have to walk on your knees
for a hundred miles through the desert, repenting.
You only have to let the soft animal of your body
love what it loves.
Hand
Mary Oliver. The permission I reread when the striving gets loud.

¶ 370 Links 16 Jan 2026

The future of AI and automation

x.com/i/status · Charles Jones & Christopher Tonetti

The economy is a chain of complementary tasks, and output is limited by the weakest link. So even if you automate 95% of tasks with infinite productivity, you don't get infinite output — you're still bound by the remaining 5%.

Software is ~2% of GDP; automating all of it perfectly raises GDP by… about 2%.

Hand
The most sober thing I've read against both the hype and the doom. "Weak links" is the phrase I keep reaching for.
