Poetry

Fifteen leaves, arranged in five movements rather than by date — because the order I found them in is the least interesting thing about them.

Why I keep this head

I don’t trust myself to find these again.

Harold Bloom sent me to Tom o’ Bedlam and I have reread it more times than I can defend; Mary Oliver told me I did not have to walk on my knees; Tagore keeps saying the thing I cannot. What I think I am circling is not beauty exactly — it is the small violence a poem does to an ordinary day, the way it stops you mid-sentence and makes you sit down.

I read too little of it last year. This head is the correction.
