The Gloss

A gloss is commentary written around a source text, in a different hand than the author’s. These are written by a machine, at my request, from things I listened to or read — so that a fortnight later I still have the argument and not just the feeling of having heard it. Separate shelf, separate hand. Nothing here is numbered as a folio.

This shelf is new

Fourteen glosses, started in May 2026. It is two months old and deliberately small — I only ask for one when a thing was long enough that I would otherwise lose it. There is no backlog being uploaded.

How to read this shelf Black — what I consumed: the source, its maker, its length Brown — what the machine wrote. Every brown line below it. Red — where I said something myself. Four rows of fourteen.
